About Counter & Cupboard

Counter & Cupboard started with a simple frustration: most kitchen gear advice online is either a 4,000-word list of forty products nobody has touched, or marketing copy wearing a review costume.

We do it differently. Every guide on this site answers one real question people actually ask about their kitchens and homes — cast iron or nonstick? is a stand mixer worth it? which pantry containers survive real life? — with a clear recommendation and honest reasoning. We keep guides short, update them when our opinions change, and tell you when the cheap option is the right one (it often is).

How we pick

We pick gear for the job it is built to do: how it cooks, how it cleans, how it stores, and whether it keeps earning its spot. These are researched recommendations, not a hands-on test. We have not cooked with every item on the site.

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links, including links to Amazon. If you buy through them, we earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.
