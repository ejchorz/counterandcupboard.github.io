Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Counter & Cupboard ("we," "this site") respects your privacy. This page explains what information is collected when you visit and how it's used.

Information we collect

This is a static content website. We do not require accounts, and we do not collect names, addresses, or payment information. Information that may be collected includes:

These cookies are governed by the destination site's own privacy policy — for Amazon, see the Amazon Privacy Notice on Amazon.com.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade any visitor information. Data handled by our hosting or analytics providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy
